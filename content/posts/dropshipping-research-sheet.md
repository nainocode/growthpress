## Score each idea in public data

Track search intent keywords, average retail price, estimated COGS, shipping weight class, and return horror stories in forums. Color-code red flags.

## Sample math that matters

Model contribution margin after ads at conservative conversion rates. If Facebook CPM swings destroy margin, flag the concept early.

Revisit the sheet weekly; patterns emerge faster than gut feelings.
