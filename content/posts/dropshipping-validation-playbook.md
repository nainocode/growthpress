## Validate before buying inventory

Use no-code landing pages and pre-launch interest tracking to avoid dead-stock mistakes.

## Focus on offer-market fit

Price, delivery speed, and perceived utility matter more than product novelty.

### Metrics that matter

Track CTR, add-to-cart rate, and return-on-ad-spend by creative angle.
