## Editorial micro-audits

Confirm title uniqueness, descriptive meta under 155 characters, H2 hierarchy reflecting reader questions, descriptive alt text, internal links outward and inward, HTTPS assets, crawlable routes, canonical tags, FAQ schema optional, snippet-friendly summaries.

## Humans first

Readable intros beat dense jargon. Satisfy scanners with bullets but keep persuasive narrative for decisive segments.
