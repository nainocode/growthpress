## 1. ChatGPT – Your All-in-One Productivity Assistant

ChatGPT is one of the most powerful AI tools for writing, planning, coding, and problem-solving.

### What you can do:
- Write blogs, emails, and scripts  
- Generate business ideas  
- Solve coding problems  
- Create marketing strategies  

### Example:
You can ask:

And get a complete structured answer in seconds.

Use ChatGPT as your daily assistant for planning tasks and writing content faster.
