## Quarterly spine

Draft three evergreen pillars rotated weekly. Week four each month repurposes transcripts, threads, older posts into condensed threads or carousels off-site.

Monitor decay: revisit pages slipping in impressions quarterly with refreshed intros and internal linking.

Celebrate small metrics—dwell time deltas, revived returning readers—beyond raw sessions alone.
