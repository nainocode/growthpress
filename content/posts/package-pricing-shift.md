## Start with value mapping

Interview past clients for moments they felt relief. Translate those into named modules—Audit Sprint, Build Sprint, Stabilize Week—each with defined inputs/outputs.

## Guard with boundaries

Clearly state revisions cap, SLA response windows, and optional retainers afterward. Transparency prevents resentment.
