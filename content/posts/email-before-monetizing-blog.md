## Rent vs own

Social platforms amplify reach but never guarantee delivery. Lightweight email tooling plus double opt-ins creates a transferable asset—even if monetization waits until you sharpen positioning.

Treat every article as onboarding: tuck a contextual lead magnet referencing the exact playbook inside the paragraph where readers binge.

## Sequencing

Batch three educational emails referencing older posts before pitching anything. Credibility climbs when readers replay your evergreen notes from their inbox—not from a hurried DM.
