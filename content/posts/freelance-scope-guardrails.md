## Start with a living scope doc

Before wireframes, agree on must-have success metrics, channel owners, and review cadence. Surface unknowns as explicit discovery tasks with capped hours.

## Change requests with receipts

When requirements shift, respond with a short diff plus impact on timeline. Clients respect boundaries when you pair empathy with numbers.

Use async Loom recaps after major meetings so everyone references the same promises.
