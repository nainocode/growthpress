## Separate facts from fluff

Draft prompts requesting bullet summaries ONLY from pasted primary sources—never accept unsourced assertions. Attach DOI-like references manually after model output.

Treat AI as paralegal-tier support: summarize, cluster themes, sanity-check readability—but editorial voice remains human.

Maintain a changelog per article documenting model assistance for transparency-minded readers.
