## Scope the experiment

Identify one skeptical assumption—pricing sensitivity, onboarding clarity, fulfillment promise—and design a clickable prototype addressing only that.

## Quality floor

Transparency beats polish: label waitlists plainly, disclaim manual fulfillment, automate receipts even if dashboards wait.

Defer infrastructure theater—auth, dashboards, cron—until you validate humans care.
