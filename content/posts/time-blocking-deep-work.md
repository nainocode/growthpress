## Transparent office hours

Publish two windows weekly when you guarantee sub-hour replies. Outside those spans, automate acknowledgments referencing next touchpoint—clients relax when expectations match reality.

Batch creative work contiguously—90 minutes minimum—to protect context switching taxes.

Audit meetings monthly; convert recurring syncs into written updates wherever possible.
