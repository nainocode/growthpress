## Stack your proof

Pick two adjacent skills—say React plus accessible copy—and document every deliverable with before/after metrics. Prospects stop price shopping when you surface evidence they cannot fake.

## Talk outcomes

Replace “I build websites” with “I reduce booking friction for dental clinics in under three weeks.” Specificity filters noise and shortens sales cycles.

Drop one proof asset per month into a public case study even if anonymized. Consistency signals operational maturity.
