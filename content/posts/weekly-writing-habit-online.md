## Why weekly beats daily

Posting five times sounds heroic until burnout arrives. Weekly publishing gives you runway to iterate hooks, thumbnails, internal links—without starving your drafts folder.

Start with four repeating moves: idea capture Mondays, outlining Tuesdays, drafting Wednesdays in one deep block, and publishing Fridays with a ruthless edit pass.

## The Sunday reset

Reserve one hour reviewing analytics you actually track: scroll depth proxies, CTR on recent titles, outbound clicks. Decide which topic pillar needs reinforcement next quarter and slot it into calendar notes.

Momentum compounds faster when receipts feel visible. Keep a spreadsheet that logs publish date plus one sentence on what hypothesis you validated.
